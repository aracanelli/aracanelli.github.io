Privacy policy

Family Hub is a private family calendar used only by the members of one household. It is not offered to the public.

What it accesses

When a family member connects their Google account, Family Hub reads that account's email address, calendar list and calendar events, and creates, updates and deletes events in that member's calendars when the family asks it to (for example, adding an event made on the family's wall display). It requests only the Google Calendar permissions needed for this.

Where the data lives

All data is stored on the family's own computer at home. Google sign-in tokens are stored encrypted. Nothing is sent to the developer or to any third party, nothing is sold, and nothing is used for advertising. Google user data is used only to show and keep the family's calendars in sync.

Removing access

A member can disconnect their account in Family Hub's settings, or at any time revoke access at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Disconnecting removes the stored sign-in token and that account's synced events from Family Hub.
